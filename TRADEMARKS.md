# Trademarks

The source code of DZBuild POS is free software under the [GNU GPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE). The
licence covers the code. It does **not** cover the DZBuild name or logo.

## What is a DZBuild trademark

- the names **DZBuild** and **DZBuild POS**;
- the DZBuild logo and the DZBuild POS mark (the yellow circle with the smiling bag and its torn
  receipt), including every file in [`assets/brand/`](assets/brand/).

## What you can do

- Use the names to talk about the project truthfully: "works with DZBuild POS", "a fork of DZBuild
  POS", a blog post, a review, a tutorial, a talk.
- Build the code for yourself, for your shop, or to test a change you send back to us.

## What we ask of forks and rebuilds you distribute

- **Rename and rebrand.** Pick another name, replace the logo, the icons and the installer artwork
  (`assets/brand/`, `src-tauri/icons/`), and change the app identifier and the update and support
  addresses so your users never reach ours by mistake.
- **Say it in the app.** The About screen of a build you distribute must say that it is not DZBuild
  POS and is not made or supported by DZBuild, for example: "Based on DZBuild POS. Not affiliated
  with DZBuild."
- Do not suggest that DZBuild endorses, checks or supports your build.

These rules exist so that a shop always knows who made the software that keeps its books. If you are
not sure whether a use is allowed, ask through [dzbuild.com/pos/support](https://dzbuild.com/pos/support).
