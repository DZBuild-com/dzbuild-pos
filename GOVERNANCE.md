# Governance

## Maintainers

DZBuild POS is maintained by the DZBuild team, listed on GitHub as
[@DZBuild-com/maintainers](https://github.com/orgs/DZBuild-com/teams/maintainers). Maintainers review
and merge pull requests, triage issues, publish releases and hold the release signing keys.

## How decisions are made

- Day-to-day changes are decided in the pull request: one maintainer's approval and a green CI are
  enough to merge.
- Larger changes (a new module, a change to the database, the LAN protocol, the documents or the
  fiscal rules) start as an issue or a discussion so anyone can comment before code is written.
- The maintainers look for agreement. When there is none, they decide and explain why.
- Some rules are not open to a vote: the non-negotiables in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)
  (offline first, tamper-evident books, forward-only migrations, three equal languages, nothing
  illegal) and Algerian law.

## Becoming a maintainer

The maintainers may invite contributors who have contributed regularly and carefully (code, reviews,
translations, hardware testing or support) and who follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). You can also ask: open a discussion. New maintainers usually start with triage
rights before they can merge.

## Changes to this document

Changes to governance are made by pull request and approved by the maintainers.
