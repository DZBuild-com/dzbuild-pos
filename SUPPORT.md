# Getting help

## Where to ask

| You are… | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| A shop using DZBuild POS | [dzbuild.com/pos/support](https://dzbuild.com/pos/support), and the help built into the app (press **F1** on any screen) |
| Asking a question or sharing an idea | [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/DZBuild-com/dzbuild-pos/discussions) (Q&A) |
| Reporting a bug | [a bug report](https://github.com/DZBuild-com/dzbuild-pos/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) |
| Reporting a printer, scanner or scale | [a hardware report](https://github.com/DZBuild-com/dzbuild-pos/issues/new?template=hardware_report.yml) |
| Reporting a security problem | **privately**, as explained in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) |

Arabic, French and English are all welcome.

## What to include

- **The version**: Settings › About.
- **What you did, what you expected, what happened**, with screenshots if you can.
- **The diagnostic ZIP** if we ask for it: Settings › Diagnostic creates it. It is built to leave out
  customer data, PINs and tokens.

## What never to share

Do not post your database, backups or `.dzpos` files, customer names or phone numbers, PINs, or your
shop's NIF, NIS or RC numbers in a public issue. If we need something private, we will ask for it
through a private channel.
