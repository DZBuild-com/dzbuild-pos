# Jewellery

The [Jewellery](route:/jewellery) screen gives the price per gram of each metal and the jeweller register.

## The price per gram

- One row per metal and fineness (750 ‰ = 18 carats) with its price, the date and who set it.
- "New price" saves today's price; the old one stays in the "History".
- "Recalculate catalogue prices" updates the price of the pieces sold by weight (labels, lists).

## A piece in the catalogue

- In [Products](route:/products), open the item, then the "Jewel" tab: metal, fineness, weight in grams, making charge (the price of the work), stones and hallmark.
- "Priced by weight": the till works out weight × price per gram + making charge.
- Labels can carry a "Jewel line": metal, fineness, weight and hallmark.

## The jeweller register

- Every sale of a piece is written in it with the buyer's name, identity document and address, numbered without gaps each year.
- The register is kept on the main PC: a till writes the sale there as soon as it is made, and sells a piece only while connected to the main PC.
- The "Register" tab shows it by year; export it to Excel, CSV or PDF to print it.
- Only the owner, the manager and the roles that manage customers see it: a cashier never reads the buyers' identity documents.
- Fill in the number and date of your authorisation and, if needed, the cash payment limit.
