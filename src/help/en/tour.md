# The two-minute tour

Six screens to know. Each step takes twenty seconds.

## 1. Sell

The sale screen opens for the cashier. Scan a barcode or type a name: the line is added. The amount due is in the yellow band. `F12` takes payment.

## 2. Take payment

Choose Cash, CIB, Edahabia, BaridiMob or cheque. The change and the stamp duty are computed for you. The receipt prints and the drawer opens.

## 3. The till

In the morning, open the till with the opening float. In the evening, count and close (Z). The Z prints and a backup is made.

## 4. Products and stock

In Products, create or import your items with their barcodes and prices. Stock goes down with each sale and up with each goods receipt.

## 5. Settings

The shop, the users and their rights, the hardware, the backups and the updates. Every important change is written in the action log.

## 6. Help

`F1` opens the help page of the screen you are on, in Arabic, French or English, without the Internet.
