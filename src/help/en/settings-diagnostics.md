# Diagnostic and support

## This PC

"This PC" shows the memory, the version of WebView2 (the Windows part that draws the screens), the free disk space, the start-up time and the recorded crashes.

## Sending a diagnostic

- "Export the diagnostic (.zip)" holds the version, the settings, the printer list and the technical logs (a record of what the software did).
- It holds neither your customers, nor your sales, nor your codes.
- Nothing is sent automatically: you send the file yourself.

## Contacting support

The "Support" card opens WhatsApp or the support web page the owner saved.

## Technical logs

- "Technical log detail" sets what DZBuild POS records: Reduced, Normal or Detailed.
- Choose "Detailed" only when support asks for it.
