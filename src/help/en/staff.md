# Staff

The [Staff](route:/staff) screen gathers each employee's file, their salary advances and their shifts at the till.

## An employee's file

- Every user has a file: phone, hire date, monthly salary, sales commission and a note.
- Only those who manage users see the salary.
- The commission is computed on the employee's sales before tax in the "Sales commissions" report.

## Salary advances

- "Salary advance": the amount, paid from the till or another way, and a note. The advance is recorded in the expenses (wages).
- "Repayment": the employee pays back part of the advance; in cash, the money goes into the open drawer.
- "Salary deduction": the advance is kept off a month's pay, with no money moving.
- "Still owed" is always worked out from these operations, which are never erased.

## Shifts

- Every cash session the employee opened: opened, closed, duration, tickets and sales for the chosen period.
- On a till, cash advances and repayments are made on the main PC.
