# Document settings

These settings apply to A4 invoices, quotes, notes and credit notes ([documents](help:documents)). "Preview with a sample invoice" shows the result before you save.

## Layout and texts

- "Layout": the accent colour, the logo, the A4 or A5 size and the second language of new documents.
- "Document texts": the header, the footer, the terms of sale and of warranty, in each language.

## Tax identity and rules

- "Tax identity": the activity printed on invoices and, for an auto-entrepreneur, their card instead of the RC.
- "Rules": the validity of quotes and the due date of credit invoices, in days.

## Arabic on documents

Consumer information is given in Arabic (décret 13-378): choose when DZBuild POS warns you before printing a document or a label without Arabic.

## A4 printers of this PC

- The default A4 printer, the margins, and the printer, paper and copies of each kind of document.
- These choices stay on this PC; each PC has its own.
- With wholesale, "Minimum order" chooses whether a line below an item's minimum warns or is refused.
