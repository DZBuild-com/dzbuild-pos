# Products

## Creating a product

- Give its name in Arabic, French and English; one name is enough.
- Add one or more barcodes.
- Set the selling price and the VAT rate.
- Choose the unit: piece, kg or m.
- Put it in a category.
- Tick "Track the stock" to count what comes in and goes out.

## Importing from Excel

- The "Import" button of [Products](route:/products) opens the import wizard.
- "Download the template" gives a file with the expected columns.
- "Check the file" checks every line before anything is imported.
- "Download the error report" lists the lines to correct.
- "Undo this import" undoes the last import of the day.

## Prices and discounts

- Each product has a price per level: retail, wholesale and semi-wholesale.
- A quantity price is a lower unit price from a set number of items (a carton of 6, for example).
- [Promotions](route:/products/promotions) and seasonal sales lower the price during their dates.

## Labels

Tick products in the list, then "Print labels" for their shelf labels and barcodes ([labels](help:labels)).
