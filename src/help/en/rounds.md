# Delivery rounds

The wholesaler delivers to customers on rounds: the driver leaves with the delivery notes and invoices, collects what is owed and comes back to close at the till. Open [Rounds](route:/rounds).

## Planning a round

1. "New round": the driver (a user or another name), the number plate, the van and the date. "New van" creates its stock location.
2. "Add delivery notes and invoices": only validated documents on which the customer still owes an amount, and that are on no other open round, are offered.
3. Set the order of the stops by dragging them or with the arrows.
4. "Print the route sheet" in the chosen language: the driver takes it with his documents.
5. "Load the van": the products move from the shop to the van on a transfer note.
6. "Departure": the stops no longer change.

## On the road

- Tap a stop to say "Delivered" or "Not delivered".
- Type the amount collected and how: cash, cheque (bank, number, date), transfer (reference), CIB, Edahabia or BaridiMob.
- The software refuses an amount above what the customer owes on the document.
- A stop can be corrected until the close.

## Closing the round

1. "Close the round" shows what was collected by method, the cash to hand in (the customers' stamp duty included) and the stops not delivered.
2. "Confirm the close": each collection becomes a credit payment on the customer's account and its RV… receipt prints; the cash goes into this PC's drawer.
3. If the van still carries goods, "Unload the van" brings them back to the shop.

> On a till, rounds are managed through the main PC: they need the network.

## Customer prices, minimums and packs

- Customer file, "Customer prices" tab: set an agreed price per product; the difference from the list price shows.
- Product file, "Minimum order": below it, the till warns or refuses as set.
- "Create a pack" on a simple product: a carton, bag, pallet, box or set sold with its own price and barcode, from the unit's stock.
- Settings › Documents, "Delivery note standing as invoice": with the decision's number and date, your delivery notes carry the authorisation line.
