# Purchases and suppliers

## Suppliers

- The "Suppliers" tab of [Purchases](route:/purchases) is the directory of your suppliers.
- Each record shows what is owed to the supplier.

## Orders and goods receipts

- The "Orders" tab prepares your purchase orders.
- A goods receipt can be partial: the quantities still expected stay on the order.
- At the receipt, note the lot and the expiry date of the items that have one.
- Each receipt updates the average purchase cost (CMUP) of the products, used to work out your margins.

## Invoices and payments

- The "Supplier invoices" tab records their invoices with their number.
- "Pay the supplier" records a payment, in cash taken from the cash drawer or by another method.
- "Supplier return" sends items back to the supplier.
