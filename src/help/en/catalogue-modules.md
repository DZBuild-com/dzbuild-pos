# Catalogue: capped prices, department keys and the grid

## Capped prices

In **Products**, tab **Capped prices** (Capped prices module on): the subsidised staples (milk in bags, semolina, bread…) with their legal ceiling and their current price. A price above the ceiling shows in red: fix it in the product's details.

- **Create the staple products** adds the capped-price staples (from the regulatory data) that are missing from your products.
- **Link to a regulated product** ties an existing product to its ceiling.
- The till warns or refuses a sale above the ceiling, as the sales rules say.

## Department keys

In **Products**, tab **Department keys**: each key sells a department at the price typed at the till (vegetables, fruit, sundries). Give its name in the three languages, its colour, its category, its VAT rate, whether it is sold by weight, and its place on the panel. A key that was used is archived.

## The size and colour grid

With the Grid module, a variant model's details have the **Grid** tab: the stock per size and colour, at one location or all. **Swap the axes** puts the colours in rows; the choice is kept for this model on this PC.

In a goods receipt or a stock count, **Enter by grid** opens the same grid: type each cell's quantity, then **Add the lines**.

- [ ] Create the subsidised staple products
- [ ] Prepare the till's department keys
- [ ] Receive the variants by grid
- Capped prices: under the list, the owner chooses whether a line sold above the cap warns the cashier or is refused.
