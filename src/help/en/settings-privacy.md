# Privacy

Settings › Privacy gathers what law 18-07 on personal data asks of the shop.

## The data protection officer

- Their name, e-mail and phone appear on the processing register.
- "Print the processing register" prints the list of what the shop does with its customers' personal data, to keep for the ANPDP.

## A customer's data

- In the customer's file, the "Personal data" tab records their consents (SMS, WhatsApp, loyalty) with their history.
- "Export the customer's data" saves it as a data file (JSON) and an Excel file when they ask for it.
- "Anonymise" erases their name, contact details and identifiers (NIN, NIF, NIS, RC, AI, legal form, activity, tags); the receipts, invoices and legal registers stay. It is refused while a balance, a deposit, an order or a repair is open, and while an instalment contract or a gift voucher keeps documents or a buyer's name the software cannot erase.
- Every file opened, exported or anonymised is recorded in the access log.
