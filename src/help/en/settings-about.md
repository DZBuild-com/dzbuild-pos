# About

## Version and folder

The screen gives the version of DZBuild POS, this PC's till code (terminal) and the folder where it keeps its data.

## Free

- DZBuild POS is free: no licence, no key, no subscription.
- Updates are free too ([Updates](help:settings-updates)).
- It works without internet and keeps your data on this PC.

## Grow with DZBuild

- "Create my website": a DZBuild account gives your shop a website to sell to all of Algeria (dzbuild.com/register).
- "Open the DZBuild link": link this shop to your DZBuild account to publish your catalogue and receive online orders at the till ([DZBuild link](help:settings-dzbuild)). Optional: everything works without it.
- "Read the blog": tips and guides for shop owners (blog.dzbuild.com).

## Last crash

- "Last crash" shows when the app stopped suddenly.
- "Open the diagnostic" gives the details ([Diagnostic and support](help:settings-diagnostics)).
