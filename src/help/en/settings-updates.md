# Updates

DZBuild POS updates itself, without ever interrupting a till's day.

## How it works

- Once a day, when there is Internet, DZBuild POS looks for a new version.
- It downloads it when the till is closed and checks that it really comes from DZBuild: a changed or incomplete file is refused.
- It never installs it while a till is open. It is offered to you after the Z, or at the next sign-in.

## Installing

1. "Install now", then confirm.
2. A backup is made, the app closes, installs and restarts (1 to 2 minutes).
3. After the restart, "See what's new" shows what changed.

## The channel

- Stable: for the everyday till.
- Beta: new features early, on a test PC.

## When something goes wrong

Reinstall the previous version from dzbuild.com/pos: your data stays in place. The backup made before the update is in [Settings › Backups](route:/settings/backups).
