# Zakat

The [Zakat](route:/zakat) screen makes an indicative zakat calculation on trade goods. It is not a fatwa: when in doubt, ask a scholar or your wilaya's religious affairs directorate.

## What the software counts

- The stock, at cost or at sale price, as you choose.
- The cash of the open drawers, what customers owe you and what you owe suppliers.
- The rate (2.5 %) comes from the regulatory pack.

## What you add

- The nisab of the current Hijri year, in "Nisab of the year": the figure the Ministry of Religious Affairs publishes, with a note on its source if you wish. The software never fills it in and calculates nothing until it is entered; update it each new Hijri year. Only a user allowed to change the shop's settings enters it, on the main PC.
- The calculation date: its Hijri date shows beside it (within a day).
- The bank and CCP, the other assets and the other debts.
- The calculation updates as you type: base, nisab, rate and zakat due, or "The base is below the nisab".

## Keep and print

- "Save the calculation" keeps it with a number (ZK…); it can no longer change.
- "Print" prints the calculation on A4, in Arabic, French or English.
