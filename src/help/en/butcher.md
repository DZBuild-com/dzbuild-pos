# Butcher: cutting and today's waste

## Cutting templates

In **Stock**, tab **Cutting**, a template says which carcass is cut into which cuts, and the yield expected of each as a share of the weight (leg 33%, shoulder 30%…). **New template**: give its name, find the carcass, then add the cuts. A template that was used is archived, never deleted.

## A cutting

1. Choose the cutting template.
2. Type the carcass weight, or tap **Weigh** when the scale is connected.
3. Leave the cost empty to take the carcass's average purchase cost (CMUP), or type the price paid. Choose how to share the cost: by the cuts' sale value or by their weight.
4. Type or weigh each cut: the result updates, with each cut's yield, its cost per kilogram and the loss (bone, fat).
5. Tap **Confirm the cutting**: the carcass leaves the stock, the cuts come in at their cost, and the cutting gets its `DC…` number.

## Today's waste

In **Stock**, tab **Today's waste**, find or scan each product lost, type its quantity and its reason (breakage, expiry, loss, own use), then **Save**. The day's list shows what left the stock and its total at cost.

> The costs show only to the users who see the margins.
