# Tax

The [Tax](route:/reports/fiscal) screen prepares your returns; DZBuild POS files nothing for you.

## G50 return

- Choose the month declared: each line of the G50 form (the monthly tax return) shows with its box and its amount.
- "Copy" puts an amount on the clipboard to carry it over to the form.
- Salary IRG, TAP and the other taxes are outside the till: declare them yourself.

## Registers and statements

- Output VAT by rate, deductible VAT, stamp duty, the G9 supplier statement, the annual customer statement, the sales register and the purchase register.
- Each statement opens with its filters and exports to xlsx, csv or PDF.

## Fiscal archive

- "Export the archive" saves a copy of every document, the action log and the Z closes of a period. It shows if anything was changed later. Keep it ten years.
- "Verify an archive" shows whether a file was changed since the export.
