# Invoices and documents

## Creating a document

- In [Documents](route:/documents), "New document" creates a quote, a proforma invoice, a delivery note or an invoice.
- Start from a blank page or from a receipt: "Invoice this receipt" on the receipt.

## Mandatory mentions

- Before validation, DZBuild POS checks the shop's and the buyer's identifiers (NIF, RC…).
- An invoice cannot be validated without these mentions (décret 05-468).

## Printing and PDF

- Documents print in A4, in Arabic, French, English or bilingual.
- The first print is the original; the next ones are duplicates.

## Credit notes

- A validated invoice is never changed.
- It is corrected with a credit note: a document that cancels all or part of the invoice ("Credit note" on the invoice).
