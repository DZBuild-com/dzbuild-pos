# Quick card

Print this card and keep it next to the till.

## The till's keys

- `F1`: help and shortcuts
- `F2`: search a product
- `F3`: customer
- `F4`: check a price
- `F5`: discount
- `F6`: put on hold, `F7`: tickets on hold
- `F8`: return
- `F9`: open the drawer (the reason is asked and recorded)
- `F10`: repeat the last line
- `F11`: weigh a product
- `F12`: take payment
- `+` and `-`: quantity of the selected line; `3` `*` then scan: 3 items
- `Del`: remove the selected line; `Esc`: menu

## Every day

1. Open the till with the opening float.
2. Sell: scan, `F12`, choose the payment, confirm.
3. In the evening: count the money and close the till (Z). A backup is made.

## When something goes wrong

- The receipt does not print: the sale is saved; "Reprint" from the notice or from [Receipts](route:/sales).
- Power cut: restart DZBuild POS. The sale in progress was either saved in full or not at all, never half.
- A red message at start-up: follow [Restore a backup](help:restore).
- Support: Settings › Diagnostic › "Support".
