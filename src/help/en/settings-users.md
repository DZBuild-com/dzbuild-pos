# Users and roles

## Users

- Create one user per person: the action log then says who did what.
- Each one has their own PIN of 4 to 8 digits.
- Each one has their own language: DZBuild POS shows in it when they sign in.

## Roles

- The built-in roles: Owner, Manager, Cashier and Storekeeper.
- "New role" creates your own role: tick "What this role can do".

## Security

- Five wrong PINs block the sign-in for 5 minutes.
- The screen locks by itself after the idle time you choose.
- An action beyond a user's rights needs a manager's approval, with their PIN.
