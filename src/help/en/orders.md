# Online orders

The orders of your DZBuild online store reach the till once the store is linked ([DZBuild link](help:settings-dzbuild)) and "Receive online orders at the till" is on.

## Claiming

- A new order is announced on the till; "View" opens the "Orders" list.
- "Claim": so that only one device prepares the order. Elsewhere it shows "taken by another device".
- "Put back" gives it back to the other devices.
- A "Product unknown at the till" must be linked to a shop product before selling.

## Preparing and selling

- "Prepare" once the goods are picked and packed.
- "Take payment at the till" for an order paid in the shop, or "Hand over the order" when it is already paid online.
- Cash on delivery: "Hand over to the courier"; "Ship with a delivery note" prepares a delivery note.
- The receipt or delivery note is numbered like any sale of the shop, and the stock goes out then.

## Refusing

- "Refuse", with a reason: the order is cancelled on DZBuild and the buyer is told by the online store.
- A buyer DZBuild flags is marked on the order: check before preparing.

## Without internet

- The list keeps the orders already received, and the till keeps selling.
- Claiming, refusing or putting back an order waits for internet: DZBuild must know.
