# Shop

## Identity

- The shop's name in Arabic, French and English.
- The address, the wilaya and the phone.
- The logo, printed on receipts and invoices.

## Taxes

- The NIF, the NIS, the RC and the tax article (AI).
- The tax regime: IFU (one flat-rate tax, no TVA on your documents) or real regime (TVA shown per rate).
- The price mode: incl. tax (TTC) or excl. tax (HT).
- Receipt and invoice numbering starts again each year.

## Receipts

In "Receipt texts", write the receipt's header and footer in each language.
