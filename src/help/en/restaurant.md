# Floor and table tickets

## The floor

In the Restaurant mode the till opens on the floor. Each table shows its code, its covers, the minutes since it opened and since its last kitchen ticket, and its ticket's total:

- **white**: free;
- **yellow**: occupied;
- **orange**: pre-bill printed.

"Not printed" flags a kitchen ticket that did not come out: reprint it from the print list. The floor refreshes on its own every 20 seconds.

Tap a free table, type the covers, then "Open the table". An occupied table opens its ticket (several tickets: choose one).

With the keyboard, the arrows move from table to table and Enter opens one; type the covers on the digits, then Enter opens the table.

## The counter

At the side, "Takeaway" opens a ticket numbered for the day, "Delivery" asks the address and the phone, and each predefined ticket (Counter, Terrace…) has its button. The counter's open tickets are listed below.

"Direct sale" opens the usual sale screen; Esc opens the menu.

## The table ticket

Tap the products (the daily menu first) or scan them. A product with modifiers asks them first ("Choose 1", "Up to 3"); "Add" waits for the required choices.

With the keyboard, F2 goes back to the search: type the name, ↓ picks the product and Enter adds it. Among the modifiers, the arrows move from choice to choice, Space ticks one and Enter adds the line.

- Choose the **seat** (Shared, 1, 2…) and the **course** (Starter, Main, Dessert, Drink) before adding.
- A line not sent yet can be changed: quantity, note for the kitchen, seat, course, or removal.
- "Send to the kitchen" (F6) prints one ticket per station; sent lines carry a check mark.

## Void, comp, split

- **Void** a sent line (F8) asks the quantity and a reason; the kitchen gets a cancellation ticket. Without the right, a manager approves.
- **Comp** (F5) makes the line free with its reason; the discount right or a manager's approval is asked.
- **Pre-bill** prints what to pay ("this document is not a receipt"), in equal shares when needed.
- **Split** moves items or a seat to a new ticket; **Transfer** changes the table; **Merge** joins two tickets.

## Take payment

"Take payment" (F12) opens the usual payment: cash, card, cheque… The stamp duty and the change come from the software. Once paid, the receipt prints and the floor comes back.

## Several tills

A table ticket stays on the till that opened it: it is served, sent to the kitchen and paid there. The other tills do not take it over (serving one table from several tills is not possible yet). Dining rooms, tables, preparation stations and modifiers are set on the main PC and reach every till.

- [ ] Open a table with its covers
- [ ] Send to the kitchen and check the ticket
- [ ] Take payment and see the table free again
