# Loyalty and vouchers

The [Loyalty and vouchers](route:/loyalty) screen gathers the gift vouchers, the exchange vouchers and the loyalty vouchers, and the rules for earning and spending points.

## Selling a gift voucher

- At the till, Esc opens the menu: "Sell a gift voucher" (Ctrl+3).
- Choose the amount (1,000, 2,000, 5,000 DA or another one), the payment method and, if you wish, the buyer's name.
- Paid in cash, the voucher carries the stamp duty: the "To collect" line shows it before "Collect and print".

## A customer's loyalty card

- In the customer's file, "Loyalty" tab: scan or type the code of their card, or "Generate a code".
- Every paid ticket earns them points; "Redeem points" turns them into a loyalty voucher, printed at once.
- A lost card is blocked: the customer keeps their points and gets a new card.

## Vouchers and what you owe

- The "Vouchers" tab lists every voucher with its balance, status and end date; "Reprint" prints it again.
- "Still owed to holders" is the total of the balances of the vouchers still valid.
- A voucher is spent at the till as a payment method, at once or over several tickets.

## The rules

- The "Loyalty" tab sets the points earned per 100 DA, the value of a point and the minimum to redeem.
- It also sets how long points and vouchers stay valid, in months.
