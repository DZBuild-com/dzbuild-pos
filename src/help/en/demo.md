# Demo shop

## Opening the demo

- Open it from the setup wizard ("Try it with a demo shop") or from [Settings › Diagnostic](route:/settings/diagnostics), "Open the demo shop".
- The app restarts on a separate shop named "DÉMO · Supérette El Baraka".
- Owner PIN: 2580; cashier PIN: 4826. The sign-in screen shows them.

## Practising

- It holds products with their barcodes, three customers, six days of sales and their Z closes.
- Every receipt, A4 document and label printed from it carries "DEMO – no fiscal value".
- It has no fiscal value: its name keeps "DÉMO · ", it refuses NIF, RC, NIS, AI and RIB, and it issues no invoice and no credit note.
- It joins no network of tills, never links to DZBuild and copies nothing to your real shop's USB stick: its backups stay in its own folder.
- Nothing you do there touches your real shop, and a backup of the demo can never replace it.

## Leaving and erasing

- "Leave the demo", on the orange ribbon, restarts on your real shop; its action log records it.
- "Erase the demo", in [Settings › Diagnostic](route:/settings/diagnostics), deletes it.
- You can open it again, brand new, at any time.
