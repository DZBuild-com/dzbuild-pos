# Repairs and services

Phone repairs, dry cleaning, tailoring: the item the customer leaves has its numbered slip (RP…) from the drop-off to the hand-back. Open [Repairs and services](route:/services).

## Taking an item in

1. "New drop-off" (`Ctrl+N`): the type, the customer (searched or created on the spot), the device, its brand and model.
2. The serial number or IMEI: a 15-digit IMEI is checked at once.
3. The accessories left, the condition on arrival, the fault, the estimate and the promised day.
4. "Save and print the slip": the repair slip prints, in the customer's language.
5. A deposit is offered: the stamp duty on cash shows, and the AC… receipt prints.

## Following the ticket

- The board sorts the tickets by status: Received, Diagnosis, Waiting for parts, In progress, Ready. A promised date that has passed is flagged.
- On the ticket, only the possible statuses are offered; the history says who did what and when.
- "Parts and labour": the catalogue's parts at their price, the labour at the price agreed.

## The "ready" message

- When the ticket is "Ready", the message shows in the customer's language: "Copy the message", then paste it into WhatsApp or a text message.
- The software sends nothing. Without the customer's WhatsApp or SMS consent, call them.

## Handing back to the customer

1. "Hand back to the customer" opens the till with the parts, the labour and the customer.
2. The deposit held is already taken off; take the rest and confirm.
3. The receipt prints and the slip moves to "Handed back".

## Not collected

- The "Not collected" tab lists the items ready for too long, with the deposit still held.
- Refunding a deposit needs the right to make returns.
- The "Settings" tab (owner): the "ready" message and the intake slip's conditions in Arabic, French and English, and the days after which a ready item counts as not collected.
