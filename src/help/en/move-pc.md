# Moving to another PC

## On the old PC

1. Close the till (Z).
2. In [Settings › Backups](route:/settings/backups), "Export a backup (.dzpos)".
3. Copy the file to a USB stick.
4. Stop selling on the old PC: otherwise both PCs would give the same numbers to their receipts. For a second till, choose "Till of a shop" instead ([Network of tills](help:settings-network)).

## On the new PC

1. Install DZBuild POS.
2. On the first screen, choose "Move my shop from another PC (.dzpos file)", then the `.dzpos` file.
3. The app restarts with your shop.
4. Sign in with your PIN.

## Afterwards

- Plug the printer in again and set it up: hardware settings belong to each PC ([Hardware](help:settings-hardware)).
- Choose the USB stick for the second copy ([Backups](help:settings-backups)).
