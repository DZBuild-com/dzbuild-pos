# Labels

The [Labels](route:/products/labels) screen gathers the templates, the labels to reprint and the scale items. The label printer is set up in [Settings › Hardware](route:/settings/hardware), "Labels" tab.

## Printing labels

- In [Products](route:/products), tick items, then "Print labels"; a goods receipt or a stock count offers it too.
- Choose the template, the label language, the number of copies and the label printer.
- A food or regulated item must have a label in Arabic (décret 13-378): DZBuild POS warns you.

## Templates

- "Templates" tab, "New template": the size, the information printed (name, price, barcode…) and the language.
- A template can also fill an A4 label sheet for an ordinary printer.

## Labels to reprint

- When the price, a promotion, the name or the barcode of an item changes, its label shows up in the "To reprint" tab.
- Print them, or "Mark up to date" when the label on the shelf is already right.

## Scale

- The "Scale" tab lists the items sold by weight that have a PLU number (the item's number on the scale).
- "Export the PLU list (Excel)" gives the file to load into the label scale.
