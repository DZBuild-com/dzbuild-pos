# Stock

## Where to see the stock

- The "Stock levels" tab of [Stock](route:/stock) gives the quantity of each product per location.
- The low-stock list shows the products below their minimum stock.

## Stock count

- In the "Stock counts" tab, count the items with the barcode scanner.
- When you close the count, the stock is corrected by the differences found.

## In and out

- Goods receipts raise the stock.
- Sales lower it.
- An adjustment needs a reason and is written in the action log.

## Lots and expiry

- A lot carries its expiry date.
- The lot that expires first is sold first.
- The "Expiry" tab alerts you before the date.
