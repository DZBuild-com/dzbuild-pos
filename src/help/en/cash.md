# The till

A cash session runs from opening in the morning to the close (Z) in the evening, on this PC.

## Opening the till

1. Count the opening float (the morning's change).
2. In [Cash register](route:/cash), "Open the register" and type the amount.
3. The day's cash sales are added to it.

## During the day

- Money in or out (a deposit, paying a supplier, an expense) is recorded with its reason.
- The X report shows where the till stands without closing it.
- `F9` opens the drawer without a sale: the reason is asked and written in the action log.

## Closing (Z)

1. Count the notes and coins, kind by kind.
2. The difference with the expected amount is shown; above the threshold, a manager approves.
3. The Z is numbered, printed, and can no longer be changed. A backup is made at once.

> When an update is waiting, it is offered right after the Z.

## Top-ups

- With the flexy module on, the till's "Top-ups" tab shows the credit left with each operator; a red card says less than 5,000 DA is left.
- "Buy float": the operator, the credit added and the price paid to the distributor, paid from the till or not.
- The commission per operator and per day is in the top-up sales report.
- "Flexy operators" (owner): each operator's top-up product and its commission in %, counted by the flexy sales report.
