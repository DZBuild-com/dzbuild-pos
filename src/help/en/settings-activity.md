# Activity and modules

## Shop activity

The activity template chosen at installation turns the right modules on and chooses the till screen: a butcher gets carcass cutting and the daily waste, a restaurant the floor plan.

"Change activity…" shows the 25 templates. The "Ready to use" ones are complete; the "Growing soon" ones already work, and their categories and services arrive with the updates.

- Tick "Create the template's categories and services" to add its categories and services; nothing is created twice.
- The modules and the till screen become the new template's. Nothing is deleted.

## Modules

Each module has its switch and a sentence saying what it does. A module that is off hides its screens and refuses its operations; its data stays (registers and documents are never erased).

When you change a module by hand, the list is marked "Customised". "Back to the template's modules" puts back those of your activity.

## Till screen

- **Retail**: product grid, scan and checkout.
- **Restaurant**: floor plan first, then tickets per table.
- **Wholesale**: customer required, customer prices and minimum quantities.
- **Mixed**: retail till with a Tables button (café + grocery).

> On a till, every change is made on the main PC: it must be reachable.
