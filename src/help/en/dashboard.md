# Reports and dashboard

## The dashboard

- The [Dashboard](route:/dashboard) shows today's sales and today's margin.
- It points out low stock and what customers owe you (kridi).

## Reports

- [Reports](route:/reports) gathers the reports on sales, products, stock, cash, kridi and profit.
- Choose the period and the filters (terminal, cashier, category…) of each report.

## Exporting

- "Export" saves the report as Excel, CSV or PDF.
- The file is written in the language you choose ("File language").

## Who sees what

Costs and margins are hidden from the roles that do not have the right.
