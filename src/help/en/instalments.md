# Instalment sales

An instalment sale: the customer takes the item, pays a down payment and the rest in monthly instalments. Open [Instalment sales](route:/instalments).

## Signing a contract

1. At the till, menu (`Esc`), "Instalment sale": the current cart and its customer are taken over. From the instalment sales screen, "New instalment sale": pick the items in the wizard.
2. Customer: their account balance and credit limit show.
3. Items: they are counted at the instalment price.
4. Terms: the down payment and how it is paid, the number of instalments and the date of the first. The software works out the price, the stamp duty on a cash down payment, what to take now and the schedule (the date and amount of each instalment).
5. Supporting documents: identity document, work certificate, pay slip, CCP statement, residence certificate, guarantor or another document.
6. "Sign and take the down payment": the receipt prints, then "Print the contract" in the customer's language.

> No guarantee cheque is asked for or accepted: the software never offers one.

## Following a contract

- The "Contracts" tab lists the contracts with what is paid, what is left and the next instalment; tick "Late only" for the late ones.
- On a contract, each instalment has its state: paid, part paid, due today, N days late or upcoming.
- "Collect an instalment": the amount offered is what is left on the oldest unpaid instalment; the RV… receipt prints.
- "Reprint the contract" prints it again in the chosen language.

## Reminding customers

- The "Reminders" tab lists the instalments coming up and the late ones.
- "Prepare the reminder" shows the text in the customer's language: copy it into WhatsApp or an SMS. The software sends nothing.
- Without the customer's WhatsApp or SMS consent, call them.
- "Log the reminder" keeps a trace of it.
- Under the reminders (owner): how many days before its date a due shows, and the reminder text in the three languages.
