# DZBuild link

Linked to your DZBuild account, your shop gets its own website and online store on dzbuild.com: your catalogue is published there and online orders reach the till. The link is optional and DZBuild POS stays free with or without it: without it everything works (sales, stock, Z, backups, printing).

## Linking the store

- Only the owner links the store, on the main PC (or on a single PC).
- "Link the store" opens the DZBuild sign-in page in your browser: the till never asks for your DZBuild password.
- No handy browser on this PC? "Link with a phone" shows a QR code and a device code (for example DZKM-4Q7P) to type on your phone.
- Then "Which store should this till link to?": pick it and "Link this store".
- No account yet? "Create a DZBuild account": it is free.

## What goes online

- In "What is synced" you choose: the shop's stock, the photos, in-store sales for the dashboard, online orders, customers.
- "Products online": publish the products you choose, or "All active products". Beyond your plan's limit they stay as "Draft".
- Customers go only when you turn it on: they are personal data (law 18-07), tell your customers.
- Offline, nothing is lost: everything is kept and goes when internet is back. "Sync now" sends again.

## On a till and in the demo

- On a till, the link is the main PC's: the page says so and offers nothing to set.
- The demo shop never links to DZBuild: its catalogue would reach your real online store.

## Unlinking

- "Unlink", with your PIN: the till stops sending and receiving. Your data stays on this PC.
- When internet is down at that moment, also remove the device on DZBuild once it is back.
- To link again, choose the same store: what is waiting is sent again.
