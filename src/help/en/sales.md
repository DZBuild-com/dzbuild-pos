# Receipts and returns

## Finding a receipt

- In [Receipts](route:/sales), search by number, by business day or by document type.
- In "Receipt no.", scan the barcode printed on the receipt.

## Reprinting

- The first print of a receipt is the original.
- Every reprint ("Reprint (DUPLICATE)") says DUPLICATE and is written in the action log.

## Return and exchange

- At the till, `F8`; on a receipt, "Take back items of this receipt".
- Choose the lines and the quantities brought back.
- The amount to refund is shown before you confirm.
- A manager types their PIN when your role requires it.
- A numbered return note (BR) is printed.
- An exchange ("Exchange for other items") charges or refunds only the difference.

## Why a receipt is never deleted

- The law (2026 finance law) forbids changing a validated receipt.
- A mistake is corrected with a return or a credit note.
