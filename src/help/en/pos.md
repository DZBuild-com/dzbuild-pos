# Selling

The sale screen works with the keyboard, the barcode scanner or touch.

## Adding items

- Scan the barcode: the item is added, even when the cursor is elsewhere.
- `F2` searches by name in Arabic, French or English, or by reference.
- For several identical items: type the quantity, `*`, then scan.
- `+` and `-` change the quantity of the selected line; `Del` removes it (depending on your rights).

## Discounts, customer and hold

- `F5` gives a discount on the line or the whole ticket, within your role's limit; above it, a manager types their PIN.
- `F3` chooses the customer: their prices, discounts and credit account (kridi) apply.
- `F6` puts the ticket on hold, `F7` brings it back.

## Taking payment

- `F12` opens the payment. Choose the payment method; several methods can be combined.
- The stamp duty is added for cash according to the scale; the change is shown in large digits.
- Confirm: the receipt is numbered, printed, and can no longer be changed. A mistake is corrected with a [return](help:sales).

## Your activity's modules at the till

The `Ctrl+1` to `Ctrl+9` keys serve the modules turned on in [Settings › Activity and modules](route:/settings/activity); `F1` shows this till's keys.

- `Ctrl+5` puts the **department keys** in place of the grid: tap a department, type the price (and the quantity), then "Add".
- `Ctrl+2` adds a **mobile top-up**: the operator is guessed from the number.
- A phone tracked by **IMEI** asks one number per piece; `Ctrl+4` opens it again for the selected line.
- At "Take payment", a **controlled medicine** asks for the prescription and a **jewel** for the buyer's identity when the customer's file lacks it.
- At the payment, "Voucher" reads a gift voucher, a deposit or the customer's store credit; your payment types (CCP direct debit…) follow the usual methods, and the loyalty points earned are shown.
- A scanned loyalty card chooses the customer; the receipt prints in the language of their file.
- Menu (`Esc`) › "Sell from the van" sells a van's stock on its round until "Back to the shop".
- On a till, an IMEI phone, a top-up, a gift voucher or a deposit is recorded on the main PC as soon as the sale is made: the till sells them only while connected to the main PC.

## When the printer does not answer

The sale is saved anyway. A notice offers "Reprint"; check the paper and the cable, then [Settings › Hardware](route:/settings/hardware).
