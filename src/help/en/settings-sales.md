# Sales and cash

The sales rules of the whole shop. Nothing changes before "Save the rules".

## Taking payment and stamp duty

- "Maximum change": the change given back never goes above this amount.
- "Rounding of totals paid in cash": to the nearest 5 DA or 10 DA, or no rounding.
- A cheque payment can require a named customer.
- The stamp duty is added to the amount due, paid by the shop, or not computed when your shop is exempt.

## Cash close

- "Accepted difference at the close": above it, the close needs a manager's approval.
- The blind close has the cashier count the drawer without seeing the expected cash.

## Stock and products

- "Selling without stock": allow, warn or block.
- "Expired lots": warn or block, and the number of days of the expiry alert.
- "Prefix of in-store barcodes": the first digits of the barcodes the shop makes itself (EAN-13).

## Seasonal sales

The seasonal sale periods set by the wali, 12 at most. A seasonal sale promotion applies only during these periods.
