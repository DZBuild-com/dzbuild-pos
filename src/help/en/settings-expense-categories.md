# Expense categories

## The categories

- Each expense recorded in [Cash register](route:/cash), "Expenses" tab, gets one of these categories.
- "New category" adds one, with its code, its name and its order in the list.

## The group in the register

The group decides on which line of the Z report an expense paid with money from the drawer appears.

## Archiving

- "Archive" takes a category off new expenses; the expenses already recorded keep it.
- "Show archived" shows them again.
