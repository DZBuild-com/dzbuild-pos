# Orders and appointments

Customer orders, layaways and the appointment book. Open [Orders](route:/customer-orders). The orders of the DZBuild online store are on another page: [Online orders](help:orders).

## Taking an order

1. At the till, choose the customer (`F3`) and ring up the items, then menu (`Esc`), "Make it an order".
2. The type: an order (served later) or a layaway (handed over once fully paid).
3. The delivery date, the stock reservation (always for a layaway) and the deposit: its stamp duty on cash shows.
4. A layaway needs a minimum deposit, shown in the window.
5. "Save the order": the AC… deposit receipt prints and the till is emptied.

> From a validated quote, "Turn into an order" does the same with the quote's lines and prices.

## Serving an order

- "Take a deposit" adds a payment; "Ready" says the goods are waiting for the customer.
- "Serve at the till" opens the till with the lines, the customer and the deposit already paid; take the rest. A layaway is paid in full, never on credit.
- "Deliver (delivery note)" or "Invoice" validates a delivery note or an invoice; the deposit goes on the customer's account.

## Calling an order off

- "Close" is refused while a deposit is held: refund it or keep it first.
- "Keep the deposit" (the customer gave up) needs a manager's approval: a "Kept deposit" receipt prints and the order is closed.

## Appointments

- The "Appointments" tab shows one day: a column per staff member, a row per quarter of an hour from 08:00 to 20:00.
- Click a free slot: the customer (from the file, or a name and a phone), the service, the duration, then "Book". An overlap is refused and shown under the start time.
- Click an appointment to move it or mark it done, no-show or cancelled.
- Under the order list (owner): "Kept deposits": when a customer gives up an order and the shop keeps the deposit, the deposit is sold as this product.
