# Restaurant: dining rooms, stations, modifiers and menu

## Dining rooms

Each dining room (main room, terrace, first floor…) has a code, its names in Arabic, French and English, and a grid of columns and rows.

- "Add a table" puts it on the first free cell.
- Drag a table to a free cell: the target cell lights up. A taken cell is refused.
- With the keyboard, select a table and move it with the arrow keys.
- Tap a table to change its code, seats or shape, or to archive it. An archived table keeps its history.

## Stations

A station (kitchen, grill, pizza, bar) prepares categories of products: each line sent goes on its station's kitchen ticket. A category without a station goes on the general ticket.

## Modifiers

A modifier group (sauces, cooking, extras) says how many to choose at least and at most. A modifier can sell a product (extra cheese): it is then paid, at its price.

In "Modifiers asked per product", search a product and tick the groups it asks at the till.

## Daily menu

Choose the day, add the products and order them: the ticket screen shows them first. Save to publish them to the tills.

## Settings

- Kitchen ticket language: pick Arabic, French or English for the tickets sent to the kitchen and the bar.
- Counter tickets: one button per counter ticket on the floor (Counter, Terrace…), written in your waiters' language; add or remove them.
- "Send the unsent lines to the kitchen when the ticket is paid": untick it when the kitchen must get every dish before the payment only.
- Cover charge (per person seated): the product sold per cover on a table's bill, or none.
