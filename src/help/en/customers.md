# Customers and kridi

## Customer record

- A customer's record in [Customers](route:/customers) keeps their name and phone.
- It sets their price level (retail, wholesale…) and their discount.
- It sets their credit limit; "Block credit" stops them buying on credit.

## Selling on credit

- At the till, `F3` chooses the customer.
- At payment, choose "Customer credit": the amount goes to their account.
- The credit limit is checked; above it, a manager approves with their PIN.

## Credit payments

- "Take a credit payment" on the customer's record records what they pay back.
- The stamp duty applies when they pay in cash.
- A payment receipt is printed.

## Statements and reminders

- "Statement" gives their account's movements over the period you choose.
- The ageing report, in [Reports](route:/reports), shows how long each debt has been waiting.
