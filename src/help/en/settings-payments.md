# Payments

Settings › Payments sets how the till takes payment, adds payment types to the till and holds the shop's BaridiMob QR.

## Payment at the till

- **Choose at the till** (the usual way): Pay (F12) opens the payment window. Untick the methods your shop does not take: only the ticked ones show, and the first one opens with the exact amount, so Enter validates. A mixed payment still works with the ticked methods. At least one method stays ticked.
- **One method**: Pay (F12), or Enter in the empty scan field, finishes the sale at once in that method for the exact amount, without the payment window. In cash the amount includes the stamp duty and the cash rounding. The change screen still shows, and the receipt prints as usual.
- The one method is cash, CIB card, Edahabia, BaridiMob, or a payment type based on one of them that asks no reference. Cheque, transfer, customer credit and store credit need details or a customer: they stay in the payment window.
- With a return being exchanged, or if the sale cannot be finished in that method, the payment window opens instead.
- On a till, this setting comes from the main PC.

## Payment types

- A type (for example "CCP direct debit" or "BNA terminal") is an extra button in the payment, counted as one of the usual methods: card, Edahabia, BaridiMob, cheque or transfer.
- It has a code, a name in each language, a display order, and can require the operation's reference.
- A type already used is archived: it leaves the till but stays in the reports.

## The BaridiMob QR

- Paste the text of your BaridPay merchant QR and save.
- When the cashier picks BaridiMob, the customer screen shows this QR: the customer pays by scanning it.
- The preview shows the QR as it will appear.
